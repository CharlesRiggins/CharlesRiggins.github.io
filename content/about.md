Hi, I'm Charles Chen — an AI researcher working on large language models.

I'm currently a Senior Researcher at **NVIDIA**, exploring general agentic AI. I'm extremely interested in understanding why LLMs work. That includes both understanding what's going on inside them (AI interpretability) and why the simple yet powerful scaling law exists in the first place.

Before NVIDIA, I was a researcher at **Tencent**, where I worked on LLM alignment and studied how post-training shapes model behavior. Before that, I was a founding engineer at **DeepLang AI**, a startup founded in 2023. We pretrained a 70-billion-parameter dense Transformer model, and I handled its inference optimization and deployment as well (as a typical startup all-rounder would do). The inference work led me to contribute to multiple open-source projects such as vLLM, SGLang, and LMCache (where I ended up becoming a core committer).

I studied Computer Science at **Peking University**, where I received a bachelor's degree in 2022. I also spent a year as a visiting student at **UC Berkeley** in 2019.

I was born in China and my Chinese name is 陈立千 (Liqian Chen).
