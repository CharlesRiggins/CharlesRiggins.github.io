Hi, I'm Charles Chen — an AI researcher working on large language models.

I'm currently a Senior Researcher at **NVIDIA**, exploring general agentic AI. I'm extremely interested in understanding why and how LLMs work. That includes both understanding what's going on inside them (**AI interpretability**) and why the simple yet powerful **scaling law** exists in the first place.

Before NVIDIA, I was a researcher at **Tencent**, where I worked on **LLM alignment** and studied how post-training shapes model behavior. Earlier, I spent two years as an MTS at **DeepLang AI**, a startup founded in 2022, where we pretrained our own LLM and handled its inference optimization.

The work led me to multiple open-source communities such as vLLM, SGLang, and LMCache (where I ended up becoming a core committer).

I studied Computer Science at **Peking University**, where I received a bachelor's degree in 2022. I also spent a year as a visiting student at **UC Berkeley** in 2019.

I was born in China and my Chinese name is 陈立千 (Liqian Chen).
